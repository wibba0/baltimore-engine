 Q3. 311 Service Equity Across Crime Levels
 Stakeholder: Mayor's Office of Performance & Innovation (CitiStat)   (computed in 86.556ms)

    lowest-crime quartile (median 3.22 vs 2.75 days). Their overdue rate is 22.4% vs 29.2%.
    Across neighborhoods, the rank correlation between violent crime and median close time is ρ
    = 0.27 (weak).

    fewer miss their due date (22.4% vs 29.2%). That pattern points to a different mix of
    request types more than to neglect, so CitiStat should monitor it rather than treat it as an
    equity failure.
